Push-Up & Sit-Up Test Calculator

Benchmark your count against fitness norms by age & sex

Your test

Do as many complete, controlled push-ups as you can with good form, no time limit.

How it works

  1. Do the test. Push-ups: as many as you can with good form, no time limit. Sit-ups: as many as you can in 1 minute.
  2. Enter your age, sex, and rep count.
  3. We compare your result against standard fitness assessment norms for your age group and sex.

Form matters more than speed — a controlled, complete rep counts; a rushed partial rep doesn't reflect true muscular endurance.

Frequently asked questions

Do as many complete, controlled push-ups as you can with proper form (chest to near the floor, full arm extension at top) without a time limit, stopping when you can no longer maintain form.
Do as many complete sit-ups as you can in one minute, with knees bent and feet flat, touching your chest to your knees or shoulder blades to the floor on each rep.
